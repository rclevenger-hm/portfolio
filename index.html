Operating model

What I optimize for when systems meet real operating conditions.

01

Make failure legible

Instrumentation and runbooks should make the next action clearer, not merely create more telemetry.

02

Prefer reversible change

Dry-runs, staged rollout, bounded actions, and review paths reduce the cost of being wrong.

03

Design for ownership transfer

A system is stronger when another engineer can operate and extend it without tribal knowledge.

04

Separate fact from aspiration

Implemented behavior, planned work, and production-readiness claims should remain visibly distinct.
