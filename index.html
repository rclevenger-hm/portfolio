About me

Production engineering without the theater.

I’ve spent my career moving from software quality into DevOps and site reliability engineering. Most recently I worked as a Principal Site Reliability Engineer at Oracle, supporting cloud and distributed data infrastructure.

My work tends to sit where software meets operations: infrastructure as code, deployment systems, observability, incident response, automation, and the less glamorous failure modes that determine whether a service is actually pleasant to own.

I prefer straightforward engineering: understand the problem, automate what is repeatable, make failures visible, document the recovery path, and leave the system easier for the next engineer to operate.
